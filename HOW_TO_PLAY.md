# 🦆 The Ragebaiter — How to Play Online with Your BF

## Step 1: Put the game on GitHub Pages (5 minutes)

1. Go to **github.com** and sign in (or create a free account)
2. Click the **+** button → **New repository**
3. Name it: `ragebaiter` (or anything you want)
4. Make sure it's set to **Public**
5. Click **Create repository**

6. Click **uploading an existing file**
7. Drag ALL the game files/folders into the upload area:
   - `index.html`
   - `css/` folder
   - `js/` folder
8. Click **Commit changes**

9. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (in the left sidebar)
10. Under "Branch", select `main` → click **Save**
11. Wait ~60 seconds, then your game is live at:
    **https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ragebaiter**

---

## Step 2: Play together!

### You (Ms. Short Fuse 💅):
1. Open the game link
2. Click **"Create Room"**
3. You'll see a 4-letter code (like `DUCK`)
4. Send that code to your bf on WhatsApp/Discord

### Your BF (The Ragebaiter 😏):
1. Open the same game link
2. Click **"Join Room"**
3. Enter the 4-letter code you sent him
4. Press Join!

### Both of you will now be connected and the game starts! 🎮

## Controls

| Player | Move | Throw |
|--------|------|-------|
| Ms. Short Fuse (you) | ← → Arrow Keys | Space |
| The Ragebaiter (bf) | A / D Keys | F |

---

## How to Win

- **Ms. Short Fuse** wins a round by reaching the 🏁 finish line before he catches her
- **The Ragebaiter** wins a round by catching up to her before she finishes
- First to win **3 out of 5 rounds** wins the whole game!

### Prizes:
- 🏆 **He wins overall** → 💒 They get married!
- 🏆 **She wins overall** → 👊 He gets a good beating... then 💒 they get married anyway!

---

## Tips
- She can pick up ⚔️ **weapons** (frying pan, rolling pin, flower pot) to stun him
- He can pick up 💐 **gifts** (hearts, stars, flowers) to slow her down
- Run into pickups automatically — just walk over them!
- Space / F key throws your item

---

## Troubleshooting

**"Could not connect"** → Make sure you typed the code correctly (it's case-sensitive). Try creating a new room.

**Game is laggy** → You're using PeerJS peer-to-peer — if your internet is slow, movement may feel a little delayed. This is normal!

**One person can't see the other moving** → Refresh both browsers and try again.
